- Named `obix-component-radiogroup` without an npm scope (the owner's decision D-102, 2026-09-29). Previous name: `@obinexusltd/obix-component-radiogroup`. The package, its version and its exports did not change with the name.
- Repository, issues and homepage: [github.com/obinexus/obix-component-radiogroup](https://github.com/obinexus/obix-component-radiogroup); the repository starts from a clean export of the OBIX monorepo (its commit is recorded in the repository's copy of PROVENANCE.json).
- A compatibility package: every export is the export of [`obix-core-component`](https://github.com/obinexus/obix-core-component) under the old name (published 0.1.0).
